Before you customise

The customiser makes your own version of this venue's seat list, seat by seat. Please read how it treats it.

  • It is your version, not the venue's. What you make here is not the venue's seat plan and not this project's published data. Check any change with the venue before you rely on it.
  • Nothing is kept. It runs entirely in your browser: your changes are not sent anywhere or saved by this site. No cookies, no browser storage. They travel only in this page's link (the part after #, which the browser never sends).
  • To keep your changes, save them as a file on your own device, and open it here again later. You can pass them to the planner.

Leave the customiser?

Your changes are not saved. The customiser keeps nothing, so if you leave now they are lost. To keep them, save them as a file on your device first.
