Before you use the planner

The planner is for your own working figures. Please read how it treats them.

  • It runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you enter here — price categories, prices, targets, people's names, which seats you choose — is sent anywhere or saved by this site. No cookies, no browser storage.
  • Your plan disappears when you close or reload this page, unless you save it. A saved plan is a file on your own device that you can open here again later. You can protect it with a passphrase, or leave names out. To share a plan, take a screen capture.
  • Names are personal data. Only add people's names you are entitled to use.
  • Estimates only. The seat map is a schematic drawn from the seat data, not the venue's seat plan, and the figures are ballpark sums of what you enter.

Leave the planner?

Your plan has changes that are not saved. The planner keeps nothing, so if you leave now they are lost. To keep them, save the plan as a file on your device first.
